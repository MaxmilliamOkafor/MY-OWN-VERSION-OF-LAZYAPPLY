Job-site sign-in

Some job sites (iCIMS, Workday) ask you to sign in or create an account before applying. The extension uses the email and password below there, so applications are filed under your own account and replies reach you.

  • Saved only in this browser. Not sent to LazyApply.
  • Where you have no account yet, the site creates one with these details. Pick a password you do not use for email or banking.
  • With nothing saved here, iCIMS jobs are skipped instead of being filed under someone else's account.

Referral email

After an application is submitted, the extension can email people at the company to ask for a referral. These settings are saved only in this browser.

When off, the template chosen in your LazyApply dashboard is used instead.

Used for {{proof_line}}. Left empty, that line is left out of the email.

Send referral emails from my Gmail

LazyApply's own referral emails need email credits, which can no longer be bought. With this on, after each submitted application the extension emails up to two people at the company from your own Gmail, with your CV attached. It takes the place of LazyApply's referral step.

  • People are found with the LeadOcean finder below, which must be switched on.
  • No setup: a Gmail tab opens with the email filled in, your CV is attached, and it is sent. Gmail must be signed in in this browser.
  • The email is never sent if the CV could not be attached. Nobody is emailed twice.
  • Your CV and settings stay in this browser.
No CV saved.
Optional: send without opening a Gmail tab

With your own Google client ID the emails are sent directly. In Google Cloud: enable the Gmail API, create an OAuth client of type "Web application", and add the redirect address below to it. Then paste the client ID here, Save, and press Connect Gmail.

Contact finder: LeadOcean

When on, referral emails go to people found through your own LeadOcean account: the likely hiring manager for the role and the recruiter who covers it, each with a checked current work email. When off, or when LeadOcean finds nobody, LazyApply's own finder is used.

  • Your API key is saved only in this browser and is sent only to api.leadocean.io.
  • Each job uses up to about 20 LeadOcean records (two short searches and up to four email lookups).

Debugger

Everything the extension does is recorded in this browser: each run and job, every click and field it fills, AI answers, requests, page errors and snapshots of the form. The Debugger shows why each job ended the way it did and exports a report to fix from. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+D.
